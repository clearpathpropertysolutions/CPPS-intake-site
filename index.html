Frequently asked questions

Before you send the details

Do I need to make repairs first?

No. You can tell us about the property in its current condition, including known repairs, code issues or deferred maintenance.

Am I committing to sell by requesting an offer?

No. Sending an enquiry is simply the first review step. You decide whether any proposed route is right for you.

Are there agent fees?

Clear Path does not charge you an agent commission for a direct purchase. Any property-specific third-party costs are discussed before you decide to proceed.

What types of property do you consider?

We consider houses, multi-family property, apartments, commercial property and land across the USA, UK and Ireland.

How quickly will I receive an offer, and when can the sale close?

We deliver your cash offer within 48 hours. Closing timing depends on the property, title and local process, so tell us your preferred date and we will discuss what is realistically possible.
